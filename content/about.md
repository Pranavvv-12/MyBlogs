### The Engineer & The Student

By daylight (and often by moonlight), I am an engineer navigating an **MSc in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at TU Delft**. Before trading Chennai warmth for the Dutch drizzle, I completed my **B.E. in Computer Science at CEG, Anna University**, alongside a **BS in Data Science and Applications from IIT Madras**.

Along the way, I spent time mentoring students as a **Teaching Assistant at IIT Madras** — demystifying Python, ML, Java, and Linux — and worked as a **Software Engineer at S2T.ai**, building scalable systems before taking the leap across continents.

---

### Beyond the Code

Beyond code, models, and research papers, I am someone who finds balance in movement and rhythm. When I'm not debugging or studying, you'll probably find me:

- 🏋️ **Lifting heavy at the gym** or clocking early morning jog miles
- 🏸 **On the badminton court**, fiercely pretending every smash is a solved edge case
- 🍳 **In the kitchen**, experimenting with flavours and trying (often in vain) to recreate the magic of Amma's cooking
- 🌍 **Planning the next travel route**, exploring new cities with a curious lens

---

### Why This Blog

Leaving behind the warmth, familiar chaos, and Sunday comforts of home wasn't easy. I'm an emotional person at heart, but fiercely driven by the conviction that growth lives right outside the comfort zone — even if it means trading Amma's filter coffee for canal-side espresso machines.

**Filter Coffee to Cappuccino** is my open notebook on this transition — balancing ambition with homesickness, surviving grad school, embracing Dutch bike lanes, and carving out an authentic life abroad, one honest cup at a time.
