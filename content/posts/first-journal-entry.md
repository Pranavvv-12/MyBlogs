## A Fresh Start

Every beginning deserves a moment of acknowledgement. This is mine.

I've been meaning to start a personal blog for years — a quiet corner of the internet that's *mine*. Not a portfolio polished for recruiters. Not a thread optimised for engagement. Just a place to think out loud, track my growth, and occasionally share something that might be useful to someone else passing through.

So here we are.

---

## Why a Blog in 2026?

Fair question. Social media is loud. AI generates content faster than I can read it. The attention economy has made genuine writing feel almost rebellious.

That's exactly why I'm doing it.

There's something different about *writing for yourself*. Long-form. Unhurried. Without a character limit or an algorithm deciding who sees it. This blog is a commitment to that slower, more deliberate kind of thinking.

---

## What to Expect Here

I plan to write about:

- **Daily reflections** — what I learned, what surprised me, what I'm still figuring out
- **Books and ideas** — notes from things I've read that stuck with me
- **Technical explorations** — projects, experiments, things I built and why
- **Life stuff** — travel, habits, the occasional existential tangent

There's no strict editorial calendar. The only rule is *honesty*.
